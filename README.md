## The pitch

> We train seagulls to fetch phones dropped off cliffs and rooftops, and phone insurers pay us per handset recovered — because a rescued phone costs them far less than a replacement.

One sentence, 180 characters. It sounds absurd — seagulls as a retrieval
service — until the second look: phones lost off cliffs and rooftops are
usually written off, insurers pay full replacement cost, and a trained bird
that brings the handset back turns a total loss into a cheap recovery fee.
The customer is named (phone insurers), the thing sold is named (recovered
handsets), and the money is obvious (per-recovery payment beats a replacement
payout).

## How it works

`main.py` writes the fixed pitch above to `/output/entry.txt`. No network,
no randomness, no secrets — the sentence is a constant in the code.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
